New Zealand — 11 Days, Both Islands

Oct 25–26Sydney warm-up

Sydney

  • Day 1: stay outside, stay awake — Circular Quay, Opera House, Botanic Gardens
  • Day 2: Bondi → Coogee coastal walk or the Manly ferry; evening at The Rocks
Jet-lag tip: NZ is 2 hrs ahead of Sydney — these two days do the adjusting for you.
Oct 27✈ 3 hr, land 3:30pm

Auckland

  • Land ~3:30pm, pick up rental car #1
  • Easy evening: Viaduct Harbour dinner or Mt Eden summit at sunset
Book: SYD→AKL flight · car #1 (AKL → Wellington one-way)
Oct 28Drive ~3 hr total

Hobbiton → Rotorua

  • Hobbiton Movie Set late-morning Signature Tour (~2.5 hr, NZ$130)
  • On to Rotorua (~1 hr); evening soak at the lakeside Polynesian Spa
Book now: Hobbiton timed departures sell out
Oct 29Drive ~2.5 hr

Rotorua geothermal → Tongariro

  • Wai-O-Tapu or Te Puia — geysers, boiling mud, the Champagne Pool
  • Redwoods forest walk, then via Taupo (Huka Falls stop) to National Park Village
Stay 2 nights in National Park Village / Whakapapa
Oct 30Hike 19.4 km

Tongariro Alpine Crossing

  • Volcanic craters, Red Crater, the Emerald Lakes — a top-tier world day hike
  • Late-Oct snow possible up high — check conditions; guides/crampons sometimes advised
  • Backup if weather turns: Tama Lakes or Taranaki Falls tracks
Book: free DOC booking (mandatory) + shuttle from the village
Oct 31Drive ~4.5 hr

Wellington

  • Waterfront, Te Papa (free), Mt Victoria lookout
  • Zealandia by Night — your best realistic chance to see a wild kiwi
Book: Zealandia night tour — small groups, sells out
Nov 1✈ 1 hr 15

Fly to Queenstown

  • Morning on Cuba St, drop car #1, early-afternoon flight — left window seat for the Alps
  • Pick up car #2; gondola to Bob's Peak, obligatory Fergburger
Book: WLG→ZQN direct · car #2 (ZQN → Christchurch one-way)
Nov 2Drive ~2 hr

Te Anau

  • Scenic drive down Lake Wakatipu; lakefront walk
  • Evening Te Anau Glowworm Caves boat tour
Staying here makes tomorrow's Milford day far easier. Book: caves tour
Nov 3Big day · drive 5.5 hr

Milford Sound

  • Milford Road at dawn — Eglinton Valley, Mirror Lakes; check NZTA status the night before
  • Key Summit hike (~3 hr) + early-afternoon cruise — spring waterfalls at full blast
  • Watch for fur seals, dolphins, Fiordland crested penguins; back to Queenstown by evening
Book: small-boat cruise · fuel up in Te Anau (none past it)
Nov 4Drive ~2 hr

Glenorchy → Wānaka

  • Morning on the Glenorchy road; walk the first stretch of the Routeburn Track (2–3 hr)
  • Afternoon over the Crown Range to Wānaka; sunset at #ThatWanakaTree
No booking needed for a Routeburn day walk
Nov 5Drive ~2.5 hr

Wānaka → Aoraki / Mt Cook

  • Short morning walk (Diamond Lake lookout — Roys Peak closed for lambing until Nov 10)
  • Lindis Pass to Mt Cook; Hooker Valley Track (3 hr) in golden evening light
  • Night: Aoraki Dark Sky Reserve stargazing
Book early: Mt Cook Village lodging (or Twizel) · stargazing tour
Nov 6Drive ~4 hr · ✈ 3.5 hr

Tekapo → Christchurch → Sydney

  • Optional sunrise Kea Point walk; Lake Tekapo stop (Church of the Good Shepherd)
  • Drop car in Christchurch by ~4pm; ~6pm flight, lands Sydney ~7:45pm
  • Sat Nov 7: fly home from Sydney, 11:20am
Book: CHC→SYD flight · Sydney airport hotel for Nov 6
Weather flex: Tongariro (Oct 30) and Milford (Nov 3) are the weather-sensitive days — both have built-in backups, and Milford can swap with the Glenorchy morning if the forecast disagrees. Driving: left side; real times run ~20% over the map apps. Budget ballpark: ~NZ$3,500–4,500 per person excluding international flights.
